Academic background

My master’s thesis in progress and publications on reconciliation, stigma and peacebuilding in Colombia.

Master’s thesis

Moral decisions about punishment and compensation in the context of discrimination against signatories of the Peace Agreement in Colombia

MSc in Psychology · Universidad de los Andes · Supervisor: William Jiménez-Leal

Central question

How are attention, moral emotions and favorability toward the Peace Agreement related to observers’ preferences for punishment and compensation in situations of discrimination, depending on whether or not the victim is a signatory of the Agreement?

The mechanism I study

Attention

Toward the perpetrator
Toward the victim
→

Emotion

Moral anger and contempt
Compassion and empathy
→

Decision

Punishment
Compensation

Simplified diagram of the theoretical model.

Design

Experiment 1 · laboratory

Attention, emotion and decision

Eye tracking with news-based trials across different situations and victim conditions.

Experiment 2 · field

Simplified replication

Self-report questionnaire, without eye-tracking calibration, to gain statistical power.

Status

Thesis proposal in development and two pilots completed. The results report from the pilots is published (in Spanish).
