Professional profile

Psychologist trained in the behavioral sciences (MSc in Psychology in progress, Universidad de los Andes) with more than eight years of turning data into decisions. My work is organized around three mutually reinforcing areas: data analysis (instrument design, quantitative and qualitative analysis, modeling and dashboards with Power BI/DAX, Python, R and SQL); policy and program evaluation and research in high-level academic settings, with monitoring systems and evaluation frameworks; and measurement in educational contexts, where I took part in national education quality measurement exercises, including fieldwork and the application of qualitative and quantitative instruments.

I took part in the needs-identification process for the design of master’s programs in the Department of Psychology at Universidad de los Andes.

I am interested in contributing to solving problems in settings that require information-based decisions, and to the design of programs and policies with high social impact.
